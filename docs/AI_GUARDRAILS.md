# AI Guardrails

AI is optional and disabled without `OPENAI_API_KEY`.

Allowed AI behavior:

- Translate customer language into structured search filters.
- Tag uploaded image inputs with broad category, color, style, silhouette and material group.
- Summarize validated comparison JSON.

Disallowed AI behavior:

- Invent products.
- Invent dimensions, prices, materials or availability.
- Validate configurator rules.
- Send room photos to AI before explicit consent.
