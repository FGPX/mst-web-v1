# Musterring Product Advisor

**Ask Musterring** opens a warm-ivory desktop side panel or mobile full-height bottom sheet. It is limited to Musterring product discovery, materials, configuration, room planning, fit guidance, saved projects and retailer preparation.

Context includes the current route/product plus structured session references to Product IDs, project/configuration/material IDs, filters and approved preferences. Raw messages remain in component memory. New/Clear conversation deletes structured session memory.

Answers can include grounded facts, product cards, material cards, comparisons and action proposals. Application services—not generated text—execute saves and navigation. Important actions require confirmation. Retailer preparation produces a checklist and opens handover review; it never submits.

Unknown questions receive: “Information is not currently available in the connected product data.”
