# AI Material & Care Advisor

The Material Explorer accepts a household description and extracts family, pet, use, sunlight, cleaning, colour and material-group preferences. Recommendations are scored from the bundled illustrative planning profiles for durability, easy care, pets, families and light sensitivity. These fields make the demo flows complete but remain explicitly labelled as derived planning data until supplier records for the exact covers are connected.

Each recommendation shows its Material ID, recorded characteristics, compatible catalogue products, care instructions, save/sample actions and deterministic configuration compatibility before applying. Avoidance explanations identify recorded cautions.

The advisor never adds stain-proof, scratch-proof, indestructible or allergy-safe claims. Official care-document links are labelled unavailable until connected.
